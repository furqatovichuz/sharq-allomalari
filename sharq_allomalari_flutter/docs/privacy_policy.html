Sharq Allomalari — Maxfiylik siyosati

Kuchga kirgan sana: 2026-yil 21-sentabr

"Sharq Allomalari" mobil ilovasi ("Ilova") — O'rta asr Sharq astronomlari, matematiklari va mutafakkirlari haqida ma'lumot beruvchi ta'lim ilovasidir. Ushbu hujjat Ilovadan foydalanganda qanday ma'lumotlar qayta ishlanishini tushuntiradi.

1. Biz yig'maydigan ma'lumotlar

Ilova hisob yaratishni talab qilmaydi va sizdan ism, email, telefon raqami, joylashuv, kontaktlar, rasm yoki boshqa shaxsiy ma'lumotlarni so'ramaydi va yig'maydi. Ilovada reklama yo'q va ma'lumotlaringiz reklama maqsadida ishlatilmaydi yoki sotilmaydi.

2. Qurilmangizda saqlanadigan ma'lumotlar

Ilova kontenti (olimlar, mavzular, lug'at va test savollari) Ilova ichida oldindan saqlangan va internetsiz ishlaydi. Ilova quyidagilarni faqat sizning qurilmangizda (lokal tarzda) saqlaydi:

  • Siz "saralangan" deb belgilagan olimlar ro'yxati;
  • Ilova sozlamalari.

Ushbu ma'lumotlar qurilmangizdan tashqariga yuborilmaydi va biz uni ko'ra olmaymiz.

3. Anonim analitika va xatolik hisobotlari (Firebase)

Ilovani barqaror ishlashini ta'minlash va yaxshilash maqsadida Ilova Google tomonidan taqdim etiladigan Firebase Analytics va Firebase Crashlytics xizmatlaridan foydalanadi. Ular quyidagi anonim ma'lumotlarni yig'ishi mumkin:

  • Foydalanish ma'lumotlari: ochilgan ekranlar va Ilova bilan o'zaro ta'sir (masalan, qaysi bo'lim ko'rilgani);
  • Qurilma va Ilova ma'lumotlari: qurilma modeli, operatsion tizim versiyasi, Ilova versiyasi, til va taxminiy mintaqa;
  • Ilova nusxasiga tayinlangan tasodifiy identifikator (Firebase installation ID / Analytics instance ID);
  • Ilova ishdan chiqqanda (crash) texnik xatolik hisoboti va ish faoliyati ko'rsatkichlari.

Bu ma'lumotlar sizning shaxsingiz bilan bog'lanmaydi, kuzatuv (tracking) uchun ishlatilmaydi va reklama maqsadida foydalanilmaydi. Ular faqat Ilova sifatini oshirish va xatoliklarni tuzatish uchun ishlatiladi. Google bu ma'lumotlarni qanday qayta ishlashi haqida: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. Ruxsatlar va ulashish

Ilova "Ulashish" funksiyasidan foydalanganda operatsion tizimning standart oynasini ochadi — bu orqali siz o'zingiz tanlagan ilova (Telegram, WhatsApp va h.k.) bilan matn ulashasiz. Bu jarayonda hech qanday ma'lumot bizga yuborilmaydi.

5. Bolalar maxfiyligi

Ilova ta'lim maqsadida yaratilgan bo'lib, barcha yosh toifalari uchun mos keladi. Ilova bolalardan ham, boshqa foydalanuvchilardan ham shaxsiy ma'lumot so'ramaydi va yig'maydi.

6. Ma'lumotlarni o'chirish

Qurilmangizda saqlangan lokal ma'lumotlarni (saralanganlar ro'yxati) o'chirish uchun Ilovani o'chirib qayta o'rnating yoki qurilma sozlamalaridan Ilova ma'lumotlarini tozalang. Anonim analitika ma'lumotlari sizning shaxsingiz bilan bog'lanmagani sababli ularni alohida shaxs bo'yicha o'chirib bo'lmaydi; Ilovani o'chirish yangi ma'lumot yig'ilishini to'xtatadi.

7. Ushbu siyosatdagi o'zgarishlar

Ilova funksionalligi o'zgarsa (masalan, hisob yaratish yoki bulutli sinxronizatsiya qo'shilsa), ushbu sahifa yangilanadi va yangi sana ko'rsatiladi.

Sharq Allomalari — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

"Sharq Allomalari" (the "App") is an educational mobile app that provides information about medieval Eastern astronomers, mathematicians and thinkers. This document explains what data is processed when you use the App.

1. Data we do not collect

The App does not require an account and does not ask for or collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. The App contains no advertising, and your data is never sold or used for advertising.

2. Data stored on your device

All App content (scholars, topics, glossary and quiz questions) is bundled inside the App and works offline. The App stores the following only locally on your device:

  • The list of scholars you have marked as favorites;
  • App preferences.

This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it.

3. Anonymous analytics and crash reports (Firebase)

To keep the App stable and to improve it, the App uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, services provided by Google. They may collect the following anonymous data:

  • Usage data: screens viewed and interactions with the App (for example, which section was opened);
  • Device and App information: device model, operating system version, App version, language and approximate region;
  • A randomly generated identifier assigned to the App installation (Firebase installation ID / Analytics instance ID);
  • Technical crash reports and performance data when the App fails.

This data is not linked to your identity, is not used for tracking, and is not used for advertising. It is used only to improve App quality and fix errors. For details on how Google processes this data, see firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. Permissions and sharing

When you use the Share feature, the App opens the operating system's standard share sheet, and you choose which app (Telegram, WhatsApp, etc.) receives the text. No data is sent to us in this process.

5. Children's privacy

The App is educational and suitable for all ages. It does not ask for or collect personal information from children or from any other users.

6. Deleting your data

To remove the data stored locally on your device (your favorites list), uninstall and reinstall the App or clear the App's data in your device settings. Because anonymous analytics data is not linked to your identity, it cannot be deleted for an individual person; uninstalling the App stops any further collection.

7. Changes to this policy

If the App's functionality changes (for example, if accounts or cloud sync are added), this page will be updated and a new date will be shown.
